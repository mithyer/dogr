Privacy policy

For the Dogr iPhone and iPad app and the dogr.dev website. Last changed 17 November 2026.

Dogr does not collect your data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it sends nothing to me unless you choose to send feedback. This page says what that means in detail.

What the app talks to

The app connects to the computers you add yourself. Nothing else. Everything you see in it, such as the names of your agents, the text of a terminal or a conversation, goes straight between your phone and your own computer. I do not run a server that sits in the middle, and I cannot see that traffic.

If you connect through Tailscale, Tailcat, Iroh or SSH, the connection is set up by that service or by your computer, and the data between your phone and your computer is encrypted. Some of these ways can pass through a relay server when the phone and the computer cannot reach each other directly:

  • Iroh uses the public relay servers run by n0 by default, unless you give your own relay address. A relay carries encrypted data. It can see technical details of the connection, such as network addresses and timing.
  • Tailcat uses the free relays of the Tailcat project by default, unless you give your own relay map.
  • Tailscale, SSH, your own HTTPS proxy and the same Wi-Fi use the service, server or network you point them at.

Those services have their own privacy policies. Dogr does not add anything to what they see.

Update reminders

The app asks Apple's public App Store lookup service which version of Dogr is the newest, so it can tell you when an update is waiting and show you what is new in it. The request names Dogr, the country of the phone's region setting and the language the app is shown in (so the "What's New" text comes in that language); it carries no version number of the installed app. It goes to Apple, as any App Store request does, and I do not see it. Only when a newer version exists, the app also reads one small public file from dogr.dev (/ios/update-rules-1.json), at most once a day, to learn how strongly to suggest the update. Reading that file sends no account, no device identifier and no version number from the phone; the app compares versions on the phone itself. Like any website, dogr.dev can see the address a request comes from while it answers it.

What the app asks iOS for

  • Camera, only to scan the QR code that your computer shows when you pair. No picture is kept or sent.
  • Photo picker, only if you choose a saved picture of that QR code instead. The app reads the code from it on the phone and does not keep the picture.
  • Local network, so the phone can reach a computer on the same Wi-Fi.

Feedback: the one time something reaches me

Settings has a Send feedback button. It opens the Mail composer with a message to fb@dogr.dev and two files you can open and read before you send anything:

  • dogr-info.txt: the app version, the phone model, the iOS version and language, your settings, and for each computer a few technical facts, such as how it is connected and the version of the software on it. It has no name, no address, no code and no screen text.
  • dogr-logs.zip: the recent log described above.

You can delete either file, change the text, or close the composer. Nothing is sent until you press Send in Mail. I use what you send only to look into the problem and to answer you, and I do not share it. To have your message deleted, write to the same address.

The website

dogr.dev is hosted on Cloudflare. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking scripts, and it loads nothing from other sites. The only thing it can store in your browser is the language you pick, and only if you pick one. To suggest a language, Cloudflare tells the site which country a request comes from; the site does not keep that.

Children

Dogr is a tool for people who run coding agents on their own computers. It is not aimed at children, and it does not collect any data from anyone.

Changes

If the app starts to do something that changes this page, for example push notifications, this page will say so first, with the date above. This version of the app has no push notifications.
